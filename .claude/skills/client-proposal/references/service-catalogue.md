# Datalabs service catalogue — the workshop and everything around it

The workshop is the core. These are the productised add-ons that turn a single
booking into a program, and a program into a standing engagement. Each is
separately sellable, separately priced, and reusable across any client.

Rates below are **inc GST** and follow the locked two-tier model
(`data/private/pricing-analysis-2026-08-12.md`, decided 12 Aug 2026):
public rate card = Option B, internal floor = Option A, named concessions only.

## 2. Enrolment Pack — internal marketing of the training

> **Status (2 Sep 2026): in development, not yet sold for money.** Otto's call is
> to include it free in the first Monash engagement and co-develop it with the
> client's Learning Experience Designer, then price it for L&D buyers afterwards.
> When including it free, **state the normal price and show it waived** — a line
> with no price reads as filler.

**The problem it solves.** L&D teams are measured on participation. They buy a
good workshop and then have to fill it themselves, with a stock email and an
intranet tile, competing with everything else in the inbox. Under-filled cohorts
are the most common reason a program does not get renewed — and the client blames
the uptake, not the vendor.

No training vendor markets the training for the client. Datalabs is a design
agency that happens to teach, so this is the one add-on competitors structurally
cannot match.

**What it is.** A campaign, not a flyer — built in the client's own brand:

- A **three-email invitation sequence** — announce, remind, last call — written
  to the audience, not to the L&D team
- **Intranet / LMS tile artwork** (Workday Learning, SuccessFactors, Moodle,
  whatever the client runs) at the sizes their platform actually wants
- **Teams / Slack announcement** copy and image
- A **one-page "what you'll learn"** PDF that survives being forwarded
- A **manager's brief** — a half page answering "why should I release my person
  for two hours", which is the actual blocker on attendance
- **Digital signage / poster** artwork for campus or office screens
- **Calendar invite copy** with joining instructions and pre-work
- A **30–60 second animated teaser** for the intranet or Teams channel

**Why it pays for itself.** It is priced below one extra head's worth of
group-size uplift on most cohorts, and it moves attendance by more than one head.
Say that in the proposal.

**Price:** **$1,200** (static assets only) · **$1,900** (with the animated teaser).
Delivered 3 weeks before session one.

**Upsell after delivery:** the same assets, re-skinned per cohort, at **$450** a
run once the first set exists. That is the annuity — a catalogue offering markets
itself four times a year.

## 3. Evidence Pack — proof the training worked

> **Status (2 Sep 2026): in development, not yet sold for money.** Same treatment
> as the Enrolment Pack — included free in the first Monash engagement, developed
> with the client, priced afterwards.

**The problem it solves.** This is the one clients never ask for and always need.
An L&D buyer has to justify the spend to someone above them. If the only evidence
is a smile-sheet, the renewal conversation starts from zero every year. Give them
the artefact that makes the case for them and you are not a vendor being
re-evaluated — you are the program that reports well.

**What it is.**

- A short **pre-session capability self-assessment** (5–8 items), repeated after
  the final session, so movement is measurable
- **Participation and completion** figures per cohort
- A **before/after artefact showcase** — participants' own charts, redesigned
- A **one-page impact summary** written for the buyer's manager, not for the buyer
- **Recommended next steps**, which is where phase two lives

**Price:** **$1,450** per cohort. Cheap for what it does politically.

This is Datalabs service line 2 (Learning Insights & Reporting) sold as a
product rather than a project.

## 4. Coaching Block — 1:1 follow-through

**Precedent: Texas Education Agency, 2023.** Otto ran individual PowerPoint
critique sessions booked through a sign-up sheet the client's coordinator
maintained. It filled — Ruth Ye, 18 Oct 2023: *"They are all full."* Eight named
staff booked across two rounds (INV-0434, INV-0440).

**What went wrong commercially, and must not repeat.** It was billed hourly after
the fact — USD 600 for 5 hrs, USD 900 for 7.5 hrs, about **AUD 190/hr**, against
the approved **$250/hr** advisory rate. Otto also under-consumed the client's
allocated budget and said so in writing. The client had money set aside and it
went unspent.

**The fix:** sell a **fixed block, up front, as part of the program.** The client
buys capacity; participants book into it via a sign-up sheet the client runs.
Unused hours are not refunded, they convert to a group clinic — so the block is
never a reason to under-bill.

| Block | Hours | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Small | 5 | $1,400 |
| Standard | 10 | $2,600 |
| Extended | 20 | $4,900 |

Delivered by Zoom or Teams in 30 or 60 minute slots, within 8 weeks of the final
session. Participants bring their own work.

## 5. Leave-behinds — the things that outlive the room

- **Branded template pack** — chart and deck templates in the client's brand,
  built from their real material. TEA precedent: two custom PowerPoint decks with
  geographic and list-array designs, icons and animation (INV-0440, 5 hrs).
  **From $1,200.**
- **Course seats** — self-paced course access for participants. Realized median
  is about **$83 inc** per seat; bundle at cohort size and do not discount below
  it. Sell as reinforcement, not as a substitute for the workshop.
- **The book** — not yet produced. Until it exists, it is a roadmap item and must
  never appear as a deliverable in a quote. It can be offered as a signed copy on
  publication for a cohort that books this year, which costs nothing now and
  gives the proposal a reason to be signed before the date.

## 6. Assembling a program

Sell the ladder, not the option. Three tiers, each a whole number of workshop
units, each adding one capability the buyer recognises:

| Tier | Workshop | Adds |
|---|---|---|
| **Foundation** | 1 unit — 2 × 2hr | Enrolment Pack |
| **Program** | 2 units — 4 × 2hr weekly | + Coaching Block, Evidence Pack |
| **Capability build** | 3 units — 6 × 2hr weekly | + leave-behinds, course seats |

**Spacing is a selling point, not a scheduling detail.** Weekly two-hour sessions
with an application task between them beat a one-day intensive for retention and
transfer. A Learning Experience Designer knows this and will value it — lead with
the pedagogy, not the hours.

**Check the client's procurement threshold before choosing which tier to lead
with.** A tier that crosses it turns a purchase order into a tender and can cost
a quarter. That is a scoping decision, not a finance detail.
