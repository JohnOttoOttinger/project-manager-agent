## Bands

- **German / EU corporate:** EUR 2,100–3,300 per workshop. Note these are mostly
  2023–24 and mostly onsite one-offs, not designed series — they are stale and
  low as an anchor for a 2026 series.
- **Most recent remote (ZIM, Feb 2026):** AUD 4,275 per workshop ≈ EUR 2,400–2,500.
- **Top of band held in 2026 (Marriott):** EUR 3,233 per 4-hour workshop unit,
  ≈ EUR 808 per delivery hour, against EUR 660 at ZIM.

## What justifies the top of the band

- Cohort larger than ~12
- Sessions tailored to the client's own dashboards (real prep time)
- A designed series rather than a one-off
- An enterprise buyer with budget already allocated
- Onsite within Melbourne — no travel cost to absorb, and no travel cost to pass on
