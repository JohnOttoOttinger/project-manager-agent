Questions about

Giant inflatables?

[/vc_column_text][dfd_spacer screen_wide_resolution="1280" screen_wide_spacer_size="40" screen_normal_resolution="1024" screen_tablet_resolution="800" screen_mobile_resolution="480" screen_normal_spacer_size="30" screen_tablet_spacer_size="20" screen_mobile_spacer_size="20"][dfd_accordion style="style-3" active_section="1" font_size="18" tab_title_google_fonts="yes" tab_title_custom_fonts="font_family:Arvo%3Aregular%2Citalic%2C700%2C700italic|font_style:700%20bold%20regular%3A700%3Anormal" icon_size="18" icon_color="#000000" active_two_px_border="on"][vc_tta_section i_icon_fontawesome="fas fa-wind" add_icon="true" title="What is an inflatable artist?" tab_id="1756100000001-inflatable-artist-faq-1"][vc_column_text css=""]

An inflatable artist works out what the object should be and how it holds its shape. It is 3D design work: modelling the character, testing how it reads from a hundred metres away and from directly underneath, then breaking that shape into panels a fabricator can cut and sew. Oddtoe designs the piece and hands over a finished pattern set.

[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section i_icon_fontawesome="fas fa-cube" add_icon="true" title="Can Oddtoe design an inflatable if someone else is manufacturing it?" tab_id="1756100000002-inflatable-artist-faq-2"][vc_column_text css=""]

Yes, and agencies with an existing factory relationship often work this way. Oddtoe develops the character and the 3D model, produces the client-facing renders, and delivers a pattern set your own fabricator can build from. On that arrangement Oddtoe is the design resource on the job and your factory stays the supplier.

[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section i_icon_fontawesome="fas fa-dollar-sign" add_icon="true" title="How much does a giant custom inflatable cost?" tab_id="1756100000003-inflatable-artist-faq-3"][vc_column_text css=""]

Cost is driven by height, how many separate panels the shape needs, whether it is lit from inside, whether the public can enter it, and how long it stays up. A single figure and a walk-in tunnel with lighting are very different budgets. Oddtoe quotes each brief individually — send the site, the dates, and a rough height.

[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section i_icon_fontawesome="fas fa-expand-arrows-alt" add_icon="true" title="How big can a large-scale inflatable be?" tab_id="1756100000004-inflatable-artist-faq-4"][vc_column_text css=""]

Custom display inflatables are commonly made from about 4 metres up to 20 metres tall, and walk-in structures spread further across the ground than they rise. The limit is usually set by the site: available anchor points, wind exposure, overhead clearance, and how much ballast can physically be brought in.

[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section i_icon_fontawesome="far fa-clock" add_icon="true" title="How long does it take to design and make one?" tab_id="1756100000005-inflatable-artist-faq-5"][vc_column_text css=""]

Allow four to eight weeks. Design and pattern work take one to two weeks, fabrication two to three, and freight the rest. Rush builds are possible when the shape is simple. Booking the fabrication slot early is what protects the date.

[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section i_icon_fontawesome="fas fa-clipboard-check" add_icon="true" title="What approvals, engineering and insurance do giant inflatables need in Australia?" tab_id="1756100000006-inflatable-artist-faq-6"][vc_column_text css=""]

It depends on the venue and on whether the public can enter the piece. Expect to be asked for an engineering design certificate covering hold-down ballast and a maximum design wind speed as a three-second gust under AS/NZS 1170.2, a wind management plan, and public liability cover. Indoor and shopping-centre sites also want fire-retardant test evidence against AS 1530.3. Anything the public enters or bounces on counts as an amusement device under AS 3533.4.1, which is a heavier regime again. Oddtoe works out which of these a site triggers during design, so the build is specified to suit the site from the start.

[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section i_icon_fontawesome="fas fa-sync-alt" add_icon="true" title="Can an inflatable be reused at another event?" tab_id="1756100000007-inflatable-artist-faq-7"][vc_column_text css=""]

Usually. A giant inflatable packs into crates, travels as freight, and goes back up at the next site with the same blowers. Seasonal pieces are common — the same character returns each year and stores flat in between.

[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section i_icon_fontawesome="fas fa-map-marker-alt" add_icon="true" title="Where is Oddtoe based, and can you deliver internationally?" tab_id="1756100000008-inflatable-artist-faq-8"][vc_column_text css=""]

Oddtoe designs from Melbourne, Australia, and finished pieces ship worldwide direct from the fabricator, so the studio's location does not limit where a piece can stand. Installation is a separate decision: plenty of clients use their own local crew, and where you would rather Oddtoe ran the install, travel and time are quoted up front.
