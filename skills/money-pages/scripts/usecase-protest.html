A worked example. It follows one brief - a campaign with a press day and no picture - from the first question through design, fabrication, the day itself, and what the campaign still owns afterwards. The same shape applies to any object-led action.

[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section title="How long does it take?" tab_id="1757300000002-usecase-protest-faq-2"][vc_column_text css=""]

Six to eight weeks from brief to press day for a set of four heads. Design and approval take the first fortnight, fabrication the next three weeks, and the rest is packing and freight. A rush is possible but it comes out of the design stage, which is the part worth protecting.

[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section title="Can they be shipped outside Australia?" tab_id="1757300000003-usecase-protest-faq-3"][vc_column_text css=""]

Yes. They are built to crate, and freight and paperwork are handled from this end. Cane and paper are plant material, so treatment and certification are part of the job rather than an afterthought.

[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section title="What does a set cost?" tab_id="1757300000004-usecase-protest-faq-4"][vc_column_text css=""]

Oddtoe does not publish prices. Cost is driven by how many characters, how large, how much structure each one needs, and where they have to travel. Send the brief and the date and you get a quote.

[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section title="Do we need council permits?" tab_id="1757300000005-usecase-protest-faq-5"][vc_column_text css=""]

Usually not for the objects themselves - they are carried, not installed, with no power and no rigging. The permit conversation is about the assembly or the march, which the campaign is already having.

[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section title="Can we use them more than once?" tab_id="1757300000006-usecase-protest-faq-6"][vc_column_text css=""]

That is the point. They go back in the crate and come out at the next action. A set built for one press day has usually paid for itself by its third outing.

[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section title="Can you do the animation and projection as well?" tab_id="1757300000007-usecase-protest-faq-7"][vc_column_text css=""]

Yes, and they are designed together when the brief calls for it - the same characters animated as loops and projected after dark. See projection art and generative AI animation.

[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section title="Where is Oddtoe based?" tab_id="1757300000008-usecase-protest-faq-8"][vc_column_text css=""]
