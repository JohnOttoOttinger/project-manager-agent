Six to eight weeks from brief to the night for a carved or built piece. The drawings for facilities come first, then fabrication, then the install on the afternoon of the late. An illuminated inflatable takes longer, because a specialist maker builds it from the drawings. If your late is closer than that, say so, and we work out what fits.

[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section title="What does a museum late opening piece cost?" tab_id="1757300000003-usecase-museum-faq-3"][vc_column_text css=""]

Oddtoe does not publish prices. Cost is driven by what the piece is, how large, whether it is projected or built, how it hangs, and whether it travels. It is costed the same way as any other prop fabrication job. Send the date, the room and the exhibition and you get a quote.

[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section title="Can it tour to another venue?" tab_id="1757300000004-usecase-museum-faq-4"][vc_column_text css=""]

Yes. Pieces are designed to crate, and the drawings go with them, so a partner venue can see what it weighs and where it hangs before it agrees. If it leaves the country, freight and customs get worked through with a freight agent as part of the job. Often the simpler answer for a venue overseas is to build near them, working from the drawings.

Either. It can go into the collection store and come out for the next late, the sleepover or the gala, or it can stand for the run of an exhibition and come down with it. Used a few times, one build can cost less than hiring something new for each night.
