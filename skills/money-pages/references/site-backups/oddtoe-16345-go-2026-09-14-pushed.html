Six to eight weeks from brief to press day for a carried piece. Design and approval take the first fortnight, fabrication the next three weeks, and the rest is packing and freight. An inflatable or a mascot costume takes longer, because a specialist maker builds it from the drawings. A rush is possible but it comes out of the design stage, which is the part worth protecting.

[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section title="What does a publicity stunt cost?" tab_id="1757800000002-usecase-protest-go-faq-2"][vc_column_text css=""]

Oddtoe does not publish prices. Cost is driven by what the object is, how many, how large, how much structure each one needs, and where it has to travel. It is costed the same way as any other prop fabrication job. Send the brief and the date and you get a quote.
