# Midjourney prompts

## Rules on every prompt

Same house rules as the mascot and protest sets. Unbranded and invented: no logos, wordmarks,
real products, real museums or real festivals. Nobody identifiable: people turned away, cropped,
or small. Matte surfaces, visible construction and seams, desaturated palette, overcast Melbourne
light, bluestone and brick. Append to every prompt:

```
--style raw --no signature, watermark, text, lettering, logo
```

Aspect: `--ar 16:9` for hero, timeline and video posters; `--ar 4:3` for the three drawings;
`--ar 1:1` for circles. Generate each page's hero first, then `--sref <hero URL>` on the rest of
that page so the set holds together. Where one object recurs through a timeline (the replica, the
entrance, the lobby piece), add `--cref <hero URL> --cw 60`.

For the video slots: generate the hero still, then animate it (Midjourney video, or Runway from
the still) as a 10–20 s loop with slow camera drift. Export 1280x720 H.264 and compress under 2 MB.
