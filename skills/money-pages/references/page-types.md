# Page Templates

Every page: question-shaped H2/H3s, 40–60 word answer-first openers, self-contained 75–300 word sections, visible "Updated [Month Year]", the brand's canonical sentence once near the top, one comparison table where sensible.

## Pricing page
- H1: "[Service] Pricing — [Brand]" · opener answers "how much?" with the real range immediately
- Sections: price per tier/format (day / half-day / remote), what moves the price up or down, what's included, a pricing table, 3–5 FAQ (each a question-headed mini-section)
- No "contact us for a quote" as the only answer — the page's whole point is that it answers

## Comparison page
- H1: "X vs Y: [the buyer's actual question]"
- Honest comparison — recommending a competitor's tool where true builds citation trust
- Sections: the 40-word verdict first, then criteria sections, one comparison table, "which should you choose" scenarios
- Close with the brand's related offer, one paragraph, no hard sell

## Case study
- H1: "[Client/Venue]: [outcome in plain words]"
- Sections: who the client is, the problem, what was built/delivered, the outcome with ONE quotable stat (real, Otto-confirmed), what it means for similar buyers
- Client named only with confirmed permission; otherwise "a global logistics company" style

## Credits page (Oddtoe)
- H1: "Oddtoe — Selected Credits"
- A crawlable text list, one line per project: **Project — medium, venue/event, city, year.**
- No design flourish required; its job is machine-readable evidence. Group by year, newest first.
