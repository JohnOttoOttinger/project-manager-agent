<h3><strong>Jason Burns</strong></h3>
<strong>Partner, co-head of Motion Picture Literary</strong> &mdash; Beverly Hills, California

If you only learn one name on this page, make it this one. Jason is a <strong>Partner at UTA</strong> and co-heads its <strong>Motion Picture Literary</strong> department, so the person running one of the biggest lit desks in the business is also the one carrying animation into the room.

The client list reads like a decade of the medium: <strong>Phil Lord and Chris Miller</strong>, <strong>Joaquim Dos Santos</strong> (Director, <a href="https://www.imdb.com/title/tt9362722/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse</a>), <strong>Jorge Gutiérrez</strong> (<a href="https://newsletter.oscars.org/what-to-watch/post/my-all-time-favorite-animated-movies" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Maya and the Three and Book of Life</a>), <strong>Matthew Fogel</strong>, <strong>Kyle Balda</strong> and <strong>Robert Persichetti</strong>. Films directed by UTA animation clients have taken more than <strong>$25 billion worldwide</strong>.
