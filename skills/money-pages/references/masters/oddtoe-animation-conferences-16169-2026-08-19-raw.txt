<h3><a href="https://www.cartoon-media.eu/?utm_source=oddtoe.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=animation-conferences-guide" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Cartoon Forum</strong></a></h3>
<strong>September 14th to the 17th, 2026 </strong>&mdash; Toulouse, France (onsite)

Here's one I've left off previous lists, and I shouldn't have. <strong>Cartoon Forum</strong> isn't a festival &mdash; it's where European animated <strong>TV series get financed</strong>. Producers pitch projects to broadcasters, distributors and investors across three days in <strong>Toulouse</strong>. If your series lives in a folder rather than on a screen, this is the room to be in. Worth knowing before you book: you attend as a <strong>producer with a project</strong>, not as an animator with a portfolio. <strong>September 17th &amp; 18th, 2026 </strong>— New York City, U.S.A. (onsite and virtual)

Like it's sister conference in Berlin, <strong>Pictoplasma NYC</strong> is a stand-out on this list. The artist-led feel to these Pictoplasms, along with quirky curation of imagery that the festival puts out makes me what to hit both the <strong>Berlin</strong> and the original <strong>Big Apple</strong> conferences.

This conference focuses in on characters and <a href="https://www.oddtoe.com/artist-designer/character-designer/">character design</a>. So who comes? <strong>Toy makers, animators, advertising executives, </strong>and<strong> cutting-edge graphic designers</strong>.

This year's event is held in the SVA Theatre in Manhattan. <strong>September 18th and 19th, 2026 </strong>— London, England (onsite and virtual)

The <strong>In Motion festivals</strong> are always well-curated, and this year, they are back in <strong>London</strong>. But what I like about this particular conference (and its sister one in <strong>Eindhoven</strong> below) is the platform it gives individual artists.

<h3><a href="https://www.mipjunior.com/en-gb.html?utm_source=oddtoe.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=animation-conferences-guide" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>MIPJUNIOR 2026</strong></a></h3>
<strong>October 10th &amp; 11th, 2026 </strong>— Cannes, France (onsite and virtual)

Brush up on your French, practice your pitch and your handshake, and head to the <strong>South of France</strong> where the <strong>Who's Who of the animation industry</strong> go to make deals. The first of the two 'MIP" conferences runs for three days in October. <strong>MIPJUNIOR</strong>, as the conference name suggests, is known for its festival screenings of children's programming, including kids' animation.

Go for the screenings but stay for the co-production market. <strong>Walt Disney, Jim Henson Productions, </strong>and<strong> Gaumont</strong> are just a few of the major studios that will be there.

This conference and its big brother, <a href="https://www.mipcom.com/?utm_source=oddtoe.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=animation-conferences-guide" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>MIPCOM</strong></a>, mean serious business. If you have an animation project to pitch and you have the ability to be in <strong>Cannes</strong>, you should make the journey. <h3><a href="https://www.mipcom.com/?utm_source=oddtoe.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=animation-conferences-guide" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>MIPCOM Cannes 2026</strong></a></h3>
<strong>October 12th to the 15th, 2026 </strong>— Cannes, France (onsite and virtual)

France again shows its strength on the animation conference circuit. MIPCOM is quite simply where you want to be, whether to strike a business deal for your animated property or gain a foothold in the TV industry.

The heart of the conference is the <a href="https://www.mipcom.com/en-gb/content-market.html?utm_source=oddtoe.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=animation-conferences-guide" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Co-Production Market</a>. The market has a 1000-square-meter theater and a huge networking space.  This is where co-production partnerships are explored, and financing deals are often done. <h2 style="text-align: center;"><span style="font-family: 'Bebas Neue'; font-size: 36pt;">Animation Conferences</span></h2>
<p>[/vc_column_text][dfd_spacer screen_wide_resolution="1280" screen_wide_spacer_size="40" screen_normal_resolution="1024" screen_tablet_resolution="800" screen_mobile_resolution="480" screen_normal_spacer_size="40" screen_tablet_spacer_size="20" screen_mobile_spacer_size="20"][dfd_accordion style="style-3" active_section="1" font_size="18" tab_title_google_fonts="yes" tab_title_custom_fonts="font_family:Arvo%3Aregular%2Citalic%2C700%2C700italic|font_style:700%20bold%20regular%3A700%3Anormal" icon_size="14"][vc_tta_section title="When are most animation conferences held?" tab_id="faqconf-1-2026"][vc_column_text css=""]</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">They run right across the calendar year, but there are two clear clusters &mdash; <strong>April</strong> and <strong>October</strong> are consistently the heaviest months for animation festivals and conventions. <strong>Europe dominates</strong> the calendar.</p>
<p>[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section title="What is the difference between an animation conference, a festival and a trade show?" tab_id="faqconf-2-2026"][vc_column_text css=""]</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">A <strong>festival</strong> is built around screenings and awards. A <strong>conference</strong> is built around talks and panels. A <strong>trade show</strong> is built around exhibitors and business meetings. Several of the biggest events are all three at once, which is usually what makes the travel worth it.</p>
<p>[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section title="Are animation conferences worth attending virtually?" tab_id="faqconf-3-2026"][vc_column_text css=""]</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">Some are. The talks translate to a screen; the networking rarely does. If the reason for going is to meet <strong>animation agents</strong> or studio decision-makers, in person is worth the cost.</p>
<p>[/vc_column_text][/vc_tta_section][vc_tta_section title="How were the conferences on this list chosen?" tab_id="faqconf-4-2026"][vc_column_text css=""]</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">On two things: a great <strong>festival culture</strong> for attendees, and a genuine <strong>professional experience</strong> for animators, animation agents and industry people who want to meet the decision-makers rather than just watch shorts.</p>
