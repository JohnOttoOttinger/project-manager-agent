## Who this is for
**Oddtoe** — Otto Ottinger's Melbourne experiential design studio. Physical, characterful, often funny pieces for brand events, plus owned character IP (the jackalope, "Roman General with Pinkeye", the Oddtoe TV cast). Building a China supply chain, seeded by the Havas ham-bag project, now expanding into **art toys** and **giant inflatables**.

## The region — Pearl River Delta (Guangdong), base = Shenzhen
Everything below (except one) is inside the **Shenzhen–Dongguan–Guangzhou triangle**, all ~1 hour apart:

- **Shenzhen** (base) — electronics & interactive (Huaqiangbei; Bao'an) + **rapid prototyping / 3D printing** (Bao'an).
- **Dongguan (~1 h E)** — injection moulding, plastics, **art toys / designer figures**. Towns: Chang'an, Changping, Zhangmutou, Huangjiang.
- **Guangzhou (~1 h N)** — bags & leather (Huadu/Shiling, Baiyun) + **inflatables**.
- Further afield: Zhongshan/Guzhen (lighting), Foshan/Shunde (furniture & big builds), Huizhou (electronics), Chenghai/Shantou (toy-moulding at scale, ~3–4 h), Hong Kong (logistics, ~1 h).
- Travel: Australians **visa-free 30 days** (to end 2026); direct MEL–SZX ~9h45m (~A$960–1,250 return); pay via **Alipay/WeChat Pay**; **Google/Gmail/WhatsApp are blocked** → bring a VPN or roaming eSIM.

## What Oddtoe can make
Art toys (blind boxes; vinyl / resin / sofubi figures of Oddtoe characters); inflatables (giant brand characters, walk-in environments, illuminated night pieces, wearable mascot costumes, giant food/product replicas); props & replicas (resin/metal casting, model-making); plush; enamel pins / coins / awards; premium packaging; electronics / LED / interactive installations; topiary & steel frames.

## Vision / positioning
**"A cast of funny, characterful worlds that scale from pocket to monument."** The same Oddtoe characters as collectible **blind-box art toys** *and* **building-sized inflatables** — spectacle you stand under + keepsake you take home. The moat is **owned IP + humour + fabrication**; the ham-bag proved the "spectacle + keepsake" model.

## Who pays Oddtoe
Brands & agencies (Havas/Woolworths) · festivals & event producers · museums / public art / placemaking · retail & shopping centres (seasonal installs + premium gift-with-purchase collectibles) · designer-toy collectors (direct blind-box/limited drops) · licensing & corporate gifting.

## Vetting standard (the "ham-bag gate")
Before committing to any maker, get in writing: current **social-audit cert** (BSCI/Sedex) + safety certs — **EN71/ASTM/CPSIA** (toys), **CE / fire-retardant PVC** (event inflatables). The compliance notes above come from the factories' own sites — confirm on first contact.
