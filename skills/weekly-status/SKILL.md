# Weekly Status

Build a brief status update from current local task data.

- Call `list_tasks`; do not reconstruct tasks from conversation memory.
- Lead with completed or meaningful progress.
- Call out blocked and in-progress work.
- Finish with no more than three practical next actions.
- Say clearly when task data is empty or unavailable.
- Do not change task data while preparing a status.
- When `list_lost_leads` shows lost-lead records updated in the last 7 days, add one short Business development line about them.
