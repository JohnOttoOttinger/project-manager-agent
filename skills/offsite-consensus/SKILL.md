# Offsite Consensus

Use when Otto clicks "Get us mentioned" or asks to promote a page / get reviews / pitch listicles. Brand mentions correlate ~3x more strongly than backlinks with AI recommendations, and "best X" answers are assembled from third-party listicles, review sites, and forums — not from the brand's own site. This skill writes those artifacts; **Otto sends every one himself** (banned.md rule 5 — no exceptions).

## Load first

The `geo-playbook` skill (rules, brands.md canonical sentences, banned.md). Every draft uses the brand's canonical sentence verbatim where the brand is introduced.

## Draft types

1. **LinkedIn posts** — 2–3 per new page, repurposing its answer-first sections. LinkedIn IS retrieved by AI assistants; Instagram/TikTok are NOT — don't draft for them.
2. **Listicle pitches** — find "best [category]" articles via web search (best data visualization agencies / Power BI training Australia / experiential studios Melbourne), then a short pitch email per target: who we are (canonical sentence), 2 proof points, exactly what to add. Log every target in `references/targets.md` before drafting.
3. **Review requests** — short personal emails to past clients (Clutch for Datalabs, Google for both), referencing the real project. Client names come from Otto, never assumed.
4. **YouTube descriptions / transcript cleanups** — for @OddtoeAndDatalabs uploads, carrying key terms + the canonical sentence.
5. **Forum answers** — genuinely useful answers (r/PowerBI, r/dataisbeautiful, event-industry forums) where a mention is honest; if the mention feels forced, draft the answer without it.

## Cadence

- When a money page publishes → immediately draft its LinkedIn posts + check listicle targets it strengthens.
- Monthly → one review-request batch, one listicle-pitch batch.

## Logging (the app's pipeline card reads outreach-log.md — keep the format)

- `- [ ] LinkedIn post — workshop pricing (datalabs)` — drafted, not yet sent
- `- [x] Pitch — best data agencies listicle (datalabs) sent 2026-08-15` — sent by Otto
Record every draft when created; mark sent only when Otto says so.
