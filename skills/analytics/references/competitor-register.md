# Competitor register — Oddtoe

Who Oddtoe actually competes against for work: studios and makers who would
appear on the same shortlist when a museum, festival, council or ad agency picks
three names to invite.

**This is deliberately not Ahrefs' "organic competitors" list.** Ahrefs computes
competitors by keyword overlap, which surfaced AWN, Stage32, Creativepool and The
Rookies. None of them has ever bid against Oddtoe and none ever will. Otto
flagged this himself and he is right: search neighbours and commercial rivals are
different sets, found different ways.

## The goal this serves

Otto's stated target: **be one of the three names on the shortlist** when a
museum or ad agency chooses who to invite to pitch.

Getting shortlisted is not a search-ranking problem. A producer draws on who they
have used, who peer institutions used, who won something recently, and who a
colleague named. Search is a tie-breaker at best. This register answers *"who are
we up against, and what do they have that we don't"* — positioning and evidence.

## What this set says about the gap

Four of the five are Melbourne. All five have **institutional or festival credits
Oddtoe does not**: Melbourne Museum, ACMI, Arts Centre Melbourne, Sydney
Festival, RISING, Moomba, White Night, the Shrine.

Oddtoe has one strong delivery credit — Babbling with Baobabs, Signal, City of
Melbourne, MICF 2019 — and it is six years old.

**So the shortlist problem is a credits problem, not a marketing problem.** No
amount of content or ranking closes a gap that a producer reads in one glance at
a client list. The route in is smaller commissions that earn nameable
institutional credits, which is a different strategy from the money-page work.

Also worth noting: not one of these five appeared in Ahrefs' organic competitor
list. The two sets do not intersect at all — which validates keeping them apart.

## Fields and tiers

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Known for | The one thing a producer would name them for |
| Credits | Specific projects, awards or clients |
| Overlap | Which Oddtoe capability they compete on |
| Tier | Confirmed / Likely / Adjacent |

- **Confirmed** — beat Oddtoe on a job, or bid the same job. Highest signal.
- **Likely** — verifiably does the same work for the same buyers, no direct
  encounter recorded.
- **Adjacent** — overlaps occasionally, or sits in a different city or material.

## How to find more, best signal first

1. **Who actually beat him.** The Business Development lost-lead log captures
   "other quotes in play" and who won. **Zero entries so far.** Every future loss
   logged promotes a studio here to Confirmed. Nothing else is as accurate.
2. **Credits on comparable delivered projects** — who built the last several
   Melbourne museum exhibitions and festival installations.
3. **The agencies that enquired and went elsewhere** — Taboo, Hardhat, Havas and
   Long Story Short all came through the form in 2026. Whoever they used instead
   is a direct rival, and agency case studies usually name the maker.
4. **Award shortlists** — a shortlist is a competitor set someone else assembled.
5. **Public tender and EOI awards** — definitive for public art.

## Where Ahrefs comes back in

Now that real names exist, Ahrefs is worth running **against these domains**:
what they rank for, which pages pull their traffic, what they publish. Run
content gap against `eness.com`, `ramus.com.au`, `wearesandpit.com`,
`ablanckcanvas.com` — never against Ahrefs' auto-suggested overlap set.

## Standing rule

No entry without a source and a date. A studio in here on a hunch stays
`Likely` with the gap stated — never let a guess harden into a fact by repetition.
