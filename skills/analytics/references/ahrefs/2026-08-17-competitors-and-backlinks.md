### What this says

**Every close competitor is an animation-industry directory, talent agency or
community** — Gotham Group (talent agency), AWN (Animation World Network),
Stage32, The Rookies, Creativepool, AgencySpotter. Not one is a studio Oddtoe
would lose a commission to.

This is the third independent confirmation of the same finding, after the Search
Console query data and the Gravity Forms enquiry mix: **Oddtoe's search
neighbourhood is the animation-representation world, not the commissioning
world.** Competitors, queries and inbound enquiries all point the same way.

The nearest competitors are also tiny — Gotham Group ranks for 18 keywords and
draws 116 visits. Oddtoe is competing in a small, low-value pocket of search.

## Backlinks — oddtoe.com

**440 groups of links.** Page one, sorted by traffic, is almost entirely one
fabricated testimonial repeated across throwaway domains:

> "Before Finding SEOExpress.org, I Spent Hours on Frustrating Backlink Services
> That Delivered Nothing but Cost Me a Fortune… Raised My Domain Authority From
> DA10 to DA45 in Three Months… ★★★★★"

Linking domains include `elit-agency-rank-forge.store`,
`seoexpress-ranking-hub.store`, `highascending.shop`,
`master-white-hat-and-do-follow-solutions.store`. **Ahrefs tags several SPAM.**

Anchor text is machine-generated and name-drops oddtoe.com to make the fake
review read as specific.

**First-seen dates cluster April–July 2026**, matching the dashboard's "+144
referring domains". So the referring-domain growth is entirely spam.

This explains **Domain Rating 0 against 433 referring domains**.

### Action taken

**None, deliberately.** Search Console → Manual actions reports **"No issues
detected"**. Google ignores this class of link spam automatically, and the
disavow tool is for manual actions or demonstrated negative-SEO harm. Disavowing
hundreds of domains risks catching something real for no gain.

**Consequence for reporting: referring-domain counts are meaningless for Oddtoe.**
Never present that number as a health metric. Judge on clicks, positions and
enquiries.
