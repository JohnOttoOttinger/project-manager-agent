# Content gap — Oddtoe vs the five named rivals — 17 Aug 2026

Partial. Started, then paused. Read the "Still to do" section before picking this up.

## Ahrefs could not do this

Site Explorer on the free Webmaster Tools tier is restricted to verified domains.
`eness.com` returns:

> **Domain not verified.** You can only check verified domains and their
> subdomains on a free plan.

Same for every report path, with or without the `%2F` trailing slash. So there is
**no competitor keyword data, no competitor traffic, and no Ahrefs content gap
report** on this plan — it is a plan limit, not a URL problem. Don't spend time
re-testing it.

What follows was gathered instead by reading the competitors' own sitemaps and
pages directly. It answers what they publish and how they position, not what they
rank for.

## What the four live rivals have that Oddtoe doesn't

1. **Projects as evidence, not capability.** ENESS and A Blanck Canvas organise
   around named delivered projects with a named client. Oddtoe organises around
   *roles* — Roboticist, Topiarist, Comedy Writer, Projection Artist. Those pages
   describe what Oddtoe can do. A producer shortlisting three names is looking for
   what Oddtoe has done.

2. **Named institutions on the page.** ENESS: NGV, Melbourne Museum, Brisbane
   Festival, Adelaide Festival, Fed Square, Google, NBN, Royal Children's Hospital,
   CSL. A Blanck Canvas: Melbourne Museum, Melbourne Zoo, MTC, Moomba, Bluey's Big
   Play, Gabby's Dollhouse, Katy Perry, Shaun Tan, World Expo 2020. Cave Urban:
   Sydney Festival, Woodford, RISING, Biennale of Sydney.

3. **A dated awards list.** ENESS publishes one covering 2021–2025 — FRAME
   Exhibition of the Year, Dezeen shortlist, LIT Lighting Design Awards, Melbourne
   Design Awards gold and silver, Japan Media Arts Award, MUSE. RAMUS posts each
   award as news. A Blanck Canvas goes further and **runs its own award**, The
   Puppetry Awards, with a 2026 edition page.

4. **A press list.** ENESS names FRAME, Dezeen, Wallpaper, WIRED, ArchDaily,
   Designboom, The Age, SMH, and says 50+ others.

5. **Service names written the way a brief is written.** RAMUS: "Façade &
   Architectural Lighting Design", "Stadium Lighting Design", "Public Space & Urban
   Art Projects", "Light Sculpture & Art Installation". Cave Urban splits Art /
   Pavilions / Residential / Commercial / Festivals. Oddtoe's taxonomy is artistic
   identity, which reads well and matches no procurement search.

6. **Visible team.** A Blanck Canvas has 11 individual bio pages. ENESS has a team
   page, RAMUS has artist and studio pages. This is a genuine gap rather than a
   content one — Oddtoe is one person — but it is what a producer awarding a large
   commission looks at.

7. **A process page.** ENESS `/dprocess`, RAMUS `/about/process`, Cave Urban
   `/research`. Oddtoe has none.

8. **Newsletter capture.** ENESS `/subscribe`. Oddtoe has none.

9. **QR landing pages.** ENESS has 17 (`/qr1`–`/qr17`) — someone standing in front
   of an installation scans and lands on a page. Cheap, physical-to-digital, and
   nobody else in the set does it.

10. **Authority content.** Cave Urban's Research section covers workshops,
    conferences and talks, folios and resources, bamboo, prototyping,
    collaborations. RAMUS has a "Learn" section and a named proprietary product,
    the CORE Control System.

## What Oddtoe has that none of them has

- **Topic-led search content.** "What Is Generative AI Animation?", "Brand
  Activation Ideas", "Experiential Marketing", "Animation Conferences 2026–2027",
  "Experiential Design Techniques". Not one of the five publishes anything aimed
  at search demand. Their sites are portfolios.
- **A live blog.** RAMUS stopped in Aug 2022, A Blanck Canvas never started, ENESS
  and Cave Urban have none. Oddtoe posted this month.
- **`llms.txt`.** Checked all five on 17 Aug 2026: `oddtoe.com/llms.txt` returns
  200; `eness.com`, `ramus.com.au`, `ablanckcanvas.com` and `caveurban.com` all
  return 404. All five serve `robots.txt`.
- **A generative-AI animation position.** None of the five claims it.

## Reading this honestly

The rivals win on evidence — credits, awards, press, named institutions. Oddtoe
wins on published content and machine-readability. Those are different games, and
the shortlist decision described in
[competitor-register.md](../competitor-register.md) is decided on the first one.

Content work makes Oddtoe findable. It does not put Oddtoe on a shortlist next to
Melbourne Museum and ACMI credits. Both matter; don't let progress on the second
read as progress on the first.
