# Oddtoe — media kit

For festival press offices and media-partnership managers. Every figure below is
a live read from Google Search Console and GA4 on **18 August 2026**, covering
the **180 days to 17 August 2026** unless stated. Refresh with the `analytics`
skill before sending — never quote these from memory.

**Honesty note for whoever edits this:** the audience is small. The asset being
offered is not volume, it is *position* — Oddtoe's guide is what people find
when they search the category. Say that plainly. A festival marketing manager
checks, and an inflated number ends the conversation.

---

## What Oddtoe is

Oddtoe is a Melbourne studio working in generative AI animation, experiential
design and public art, run by an artist with twenty years in illustration and
motion design and prior work for organisations including National Geographic.

Alongside the studio work, Oddtoe publishes an annually updated guide to the
world's animation conferences and festivals, and a directory-style guide to
animation agents.

- Guide: https://www.oddtoe.com/animation-conferences-2026-2027/
- Studio: https://www.oddtoe.com/
- Contact: otto@datalabsagency.com

## Audience

Animators, animation students, studio staff and industry professionals choosing
which festivals and markets to attend. People arrive at the guide **while
deciding where to spend their travel budget** — before booking, not after.

## What Oddtoe offers a festival

1. **Post-event coverage** — a written piece on the festival after attending.
2. **A listing kept current** — dates, venue, submission and accreditation
   deadlines maintained year-round on a page that already ranks top-three.
3. **Social and newsletter mentions** across Oddtoe's channels.
4. **Interview** with a festival director or programmer, published on the guide.

## The ask

Press accreditation, or a media partnership, for the 2026–27 edition.

Alternatively — or additionally — Oddtoe is available as a **speaker or panelist
on generative AI in animation**, from the position of a working animator with
twenty years of traditional craft who uses AI in production rather than
theorising about it.
