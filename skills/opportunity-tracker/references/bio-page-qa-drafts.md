## /about-oddtoe/ — 7 Q&A rows

**Who is Oddtoe?**
Oddtoe is a Melbourne artist and animator working across generative AI
animation, experiential design and public art. He has drawn under the Oddtoe
name since 1996, with professional work since 2006 for organisations such as
National Geographic.

**What kind of art does Oddtoe make?**
Two kinds: animation and physical work. The animation side covers generative
AI animation, documentary and factual animation, and character design. The
physical side covers kinetic and public sculpture, installation, projection,
prop design and fabrication, topiary and sensory garden design, and robotics.
Comedy writing runs through most of it.

**Does Oddtoe use AI in animation?**
Yes, in production every day. The studio runs a hybrid pipeline: generative
AI produces imagery and movement, rigging keeps characters consistent, and
traditional motion design carries the type, graphics and edit. Twenty years
of drawing sits underneath — the AI is a tool in a trained hand, and art
direction stays with one person.

**Where is Oddtoe based?**
Melbourne, Australia, and available for commissions in Los Angeles and
Berlin.

**What is Babbling with Baobabs?**
A live children's comedy show commissioned for Signal by the City of
Melbourne and performed at the Melbourne International Comedy Festival.
Professor Oddtoe — a scientist whose inventions include the Watermelhelmet
and the cat selfie stick — unveils a machine that lets people talk to trees,
and introduces Bab, a talking baby baobab who wakes to a nonsense chant,
demands a bucket of coffee, and answers questions from kids in the audience
live. Part stand-up, part science lesson, part digital puppetry through
real-time projection.
*(Written 18 Aug 2026 from Otto's own show scripts — BabblingwithBaobabs
Script v2 + the scripted Resolume Q&A doc. "2019" dropped on Otto's
instruction — timeless phrasing.)*

**What original stories and series has Oddtoe created?**
The Oddtoe TV Show — an animated series with a full pitch bible — alongside
Wire Taps, Date Night Confessionals, Gag Cartoonist Syndication and the
Original Stories collection, all at oddtoe.com.

**What is Oddtoe's animation conferences guide?**
An annually updated guide to the world's animation conferences and festivals
at oddtoe.com/animation-conferences/, ranking in Google's top three for
"animation conferences". It lists dates, venues and deadlines, month by
month, kept current year-round.

## /otto-ottinger/ — 5 Q&A rows

**Who is Otto Ottinger?**
Otto Ottinger is the founder and Managing Director of the Datalabs Agency, a
data visualization and consultancy firm in Melbourne, Australia. He is a data
visualizer specializing in the visual design of complex data systems, working
at the intersection of UX design, visual analytics and storytelling.

**Where did Otto Ottinger start his career?**
At National Geographic in Washington, DC, as an editor and interactive
designer — the same information design and storytelling techniques he now
teaches in his workshops.

**What does Datalabs Agency do?**
Data visualization design and consultancy: dashboards, infographics, data
tools, animated data videos, maps, digital annual reports, and style guides
for Power BI and Tableau — plus training workshops. Clients include Nestlé,
Mercedes-Benz, Bupa, BlackRock, Adidas, eBay, Marriott, CommBank and HCF.

**Does Otto Ottinger speak at conferences and events?**
Yes — keynote addresses and company workshops on data visualization,
dashboard design and data storytelling, delivered across Australia, Europe
and the U.S.A.

**What training does Otto Ottinger offer?**
Workshops including Introduction to Data Visualization, Designing Great
Business Dashboards, Tableau and Power BI dashboard design, Creative Data
Presentations with PowerPoint, Infographics & Report Design, and Visual
Storytelling for Government — in person and as online courses.
