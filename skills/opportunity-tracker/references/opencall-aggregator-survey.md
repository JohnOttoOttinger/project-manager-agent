# Public-art open-call aggregators — survey

Run 1 September 2026 for Oddtoe, at Otto's request, starting from CODAworx and
CaFÉ. **The headline is a negative result and it should change the strategy:
the aggregator route is thin and mostly gated for Oddtoe's niche.**

## What each source actually gives you

| Source | Open without login? | Volume found | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CODAworx** | **No** — listings prompt sign-in after ~1 min | Titles visible via Google only | Free account would unlock it. Skew is US/Canadian *municipal* calls (Fire Station 604, Santa Clara, Sunnyvale, Nebraska, Prince Edward County, Toronto BIA) — those are usually residency-restricted |
| **CaFÉ** (callforentry.org) | Old public listing 301s to a Creative West aggregator | US art fairs and residencies dominate | Poor fit for an Australian artist; most calls carry US state/citizenship limits |
| **NAVA** | **No** — members-only noticeboard | — | Membership decision for Otto |
| **ArtsHub AU** | Listings render client-side; category page reachable | **6** in Public Art | Subscription for detail |
| **CallOpen.art** | Titles, organisers, deadlines visible; detail gated | **6** active AU | Useful as a tip-off, unreliable as fact — see below |
| **Arthead** | 3 visible; filters paywalled | **3** AU | Thin |

Total distinct Australian calls surfaced across all of them: roughly ten, and
several were not public art at all.

## The rule this survey proves

**Never import an aggregator's summary as fact.** Of five listings that looked
live, verification at the organiser's own source killed or corrected three:

- **EMERGENCE(Y) / Science Gallery Melbourne** — CallOpen said "in preparatory
  phases, implying a rolling or future deadline in 2026". The gallery shows
  that call closed in 2024 and the exhibition has been open since 6 June 2026.
  Importing the summary would have put a false live deadline on the board.
- **Room to Move / Brimbank** — listed as generic "residency space and
  support". It is a **dance and physical theatre** residency. No fit.
- **In-site: Brimbank Environmental Art Residency** — a strong fit that **no
  aggregator surfaced**, found only by checking the council directly after the
  unrelated dance listing pointed at it.

This is the same failure mode as the `TO VERIFY` deadlines already on the
board: a confident-looking date that nobody checked.

## What actually came out of it

Five rows imported under `source_id` `aggregator-survey-2026-09-01`. Only two
are live and actionable — the Creative Australia annual grant (closed 3pm AEST
the day of the survey, so its value is next year's diary entry) and the Art
Worker Artist Grant, $5,000 AUD, closing 14 Sep 2026 and capped at the first
500 submissions.

The most useful single find is **Brimbank's annual cycle**: the 2026 round
opened Wed 1 Oct 2025 and closed 5pm Thu 30 Oct 2025, $5,000, environmental
art in Melbourne's west. That makes **early October 2026** a concrete watch
date for the 2027 round.

## Recommended change of approach

Aggregators are a tip-off layer, not a source. For this practice the higher
yield is a **commissioner watchlist** — the specific organisations that
commission projection, light, sculpture, kinetic and garden work, each checked
on its own site on a schedule. Brimbank is the template: a predictable annual
cycle, a real budget, and a fit no aggregator would have matched on keywords.

Candidates for that watchlist, none yet verified: City of Melbourne public art
and Now or Never; RISING and Science Gallery Melbourne; Vivid Sydney; Illuminate
Adelaide; Gertrude Street Projection Festival; Melbourne Fringe; sculpture
trails and prizes (Sculpture by the Sea, McClelland, Woollahra, Lorne, Swell);
garden and horticultural shows (MIFGS, Chelsea, Singapore Garden Festival,
Chaumont-sur-Loire, International Garden Festival Métis); international light
festivals (Amsterdam, Lumiere Durham, Fête des Lumières, Signal Prague, i Light
Singapore, Glow Eindhoven); and metropolitan councils across Melbourne, which
run the same shape of scheme Brimbank does.

Two aggregators worth one more look because they are genuinely open and
international: **ArtRabbit** and **ArtConnect** — both surfaced the Science
Gallery call in search and neither was tested directly here.
