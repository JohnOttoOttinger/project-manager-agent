# Track 2 — PR and stunt agencies (they commission the fabrication)

Added 9 Sep 2026, out of the competitor analysis for the Protest & Press Day page
(`Oddtoe New Growth Pages 2026/protest-page-keyword-competitor-analysis.md`, section 9).

**Different play from the table above.** The experiential agencies are targets for Oddtoe as a
**content supplier** — projection, animation, installation. PR and stunt agencies are targets for
Oddtoe as a **designer and fabricator**: they conceive the stunt and hire someone else to build the
object.

⚠ **The guide-page opener does NOT apply here.** Taylor Herring is not on page 16173, so there is no
"you're featured on our guide" hook and no UTM to watch. This track needs its own opener. The
obvious one is the work: an oversized character piece designed, built and shipped internationally,
with `/use-case-protest-press-day/` as the proof page.
