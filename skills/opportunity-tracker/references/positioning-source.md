# Positioning source — Oddtoe

The single source of truth for submissions, so an artist statement, a CV and a
press pitch never contradict each other. Reshape per form; **never restate a
fact that is not on this page.**

Gaps are marked `TO FILL`. A gap stays a gap — do not paper over one with a
plausible sentence. Curators, commissioners and press offices read hundreds of
these and a padded claim ends the relationship permanently.

## Practice, in one line

Oddtoe is a Melbourne studio working across generative AI animation,
experiential design and public art — an artist-led practice that both designs
and fabricates.

## Background — verified

- Twenty years in illustration and motion design; illustration is the
  foundational skill.
- Working since **2006**, including for organisations such as **National
  Geographic**.
- Prior practice spans political cartooning, puppetry, data visualisation and
  street art.
- Crafts currently offered: generative AI animation, documentary and factual
  animation, character design, prop design and fabrication, kinetic and public
  sculpture, installation, projection, topiary and sensory garden design,
  robotics design, comedy writing.
- Runs a hybrid animation pipeline: generative AI for imagery and movement,
  rigging for consistency, traditional motion design for type, graphics and
  edit. Art direction, project management and quality control stay in one pair
  of hands.

**This is the honest state: one strong credit, six years old.** It is the single
biggest obstacle for the gallery and museum audience, and the reason prizes and
open calls were chosen as the first rung — they are the only route where nobody
has to invite you first.

Write around this rather than dressing it up. A submission that says "one
delivered public commission, and here is the work" reads better than one that
implies more and cannot support it.
